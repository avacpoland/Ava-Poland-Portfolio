I am a junior studying in mechanical engineering at Kansas State University and minoring in Physics. I am currently a teaching assistant for the class ME 212, Engineering Graphics, a class that teaches 3D modeling and drafting skills for engineering. I recently completed a summer internship at Automatic Systems Incorporated, where I created models and drawings for various conveyor components and assemblies used for automotive manufacturing and airport baggage handling. I am passionate about robotics, automation, and mechanical design, so I spend my time at school doing as much as I can to grow and learn. I programmed and designed this portfolio to not only improve my coding skills, but also to provide others a deeper, more authentic look into my projects and passions. My goal as an engineer is to use my creativity to its full abilitiy and as one could say "max out my stats". I am constantly pushing myself to learn new skills and improve the ones I already have in order to achieve this.

Engineering Projects

  • RC Mustang Car | 2023 – 2024

    Click to view more

    This 2015 Mustang was a design project I worked on my sernior year of highschool. My goal was to create a remote controlled drift car from scratch. I altered a free model of a mustang into a shell that I 3D printed into 4 portions. I also created a spoiler for some personal flare. I then used a CNC machine to cut out the aluminium chassis and other housing for the electronics. In the end, I wasn't able to fully assemble the car because the project went over my budget, but I'm still proud of the parts I did accomplish and hope to one day return to this car and complete it.

  • KitchenAid Mixer Disassembly | Feb 2025 – Apr 2025

    Click to view more

    The kitchen aid seen above was a final project for the class ME212, Engineering Graphics. For this project, I was tasked with modeling over 50 components of a Kitchen Aid Mixer, and properly assembling them in Solidworks. After modeling, I created a full detail drawing of each component and subassembly which I then presented in a design report.
