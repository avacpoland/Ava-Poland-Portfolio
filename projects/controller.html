Design Project · Sept.–Nov. 2024

Video Game Controller

TEAM PROJECTFUSION 360KICAD3D PRINTING

Objective

Our team designed and prototyped a controller for Google Doodle games as an ergonomic, cost-effective alternative to a keyboard. The design criteria included safety, cost efficiency, compatibility, ease of use, and durability.

My contribution

I handled 100% of the CAD work, developed multiple 3D-printed prototypes, conducted audience interviews, helped test the design against requirements and safety criteria, and helped program button functions using Python.

Final design

The final controller combined a PCB, Arduino Uno R3, eight push buttons, and an ergonomic PLA 3D-printed casing. Total cost was $38.91, below the $50 project budget.

Results

Safety: 9/10 · Compatibility: 4/5 · Ease of use: 5/5 · Durability: 5/5. The controller successfully worked across multiple Google Doodle games and met the project requirements.

Design lesson: The first PCB order was too large, which forced a larger and less ergonomic enclosure. The experience reinforced the importance of checking purchased components against the physical design before committing to the enclosure.
