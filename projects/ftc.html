01 / Robot Architecture

A mechanism is only useful if the whole robot can support it.

The robot had to collect flat Pixels, orient them for the claw, place them at different heights, and complete end-game tasks. My work involved the mechanical system around those objectives, including CAD and fabrication.

02 / Drone Launcher

Precision mattered more than distance.

The paper-airplane launcher stored a compact dart-like airplane in a 3D-printed rectangular compartment. A rubber band provided the launch force, while the programming team used odometry so the robot could repeatedly reach the intended launch location.
