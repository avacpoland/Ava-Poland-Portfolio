KitchenAid Mixer Reverse Engineering

SOLIDWORKSDETAIL DRAWINGSGD&TASSEMBLY

Project

I reverse-engineered a KitchenAid mixer by modeling more than 50 components in SolidWorks and assembling them into a complete digital model.

Engineering documentation

For the final project, I produced detail drawings for individual components and subassemblies and presented a design report. The work emphasized properly defining geometry, documenting features, applying GD&T, and organizing a complete assembly and BOM.

Takeaway

This project strengthened my ability to move from physical geometry to structured parametric CAD and formal engineering documentation.
