04 / Engineering GraphicsFeb — Apr 2025

KitchenAid
Reverse Engineering

A final project for ME 212 Engineering Graphics. I modeled more than 50 components of a KitchenAid mixer, assembled them in SolidWorks, and created detailed drawings and subassembly documentation.

SOFTWARE
SolidWorks
FOCUS
Detail Drawings · GD&T · BOM
01 / Assembly

50+ components, modeled and assembled.

The project required reconstructing the mixer as a complete CAD assembly, not simply creating an outside surface. The work required understanding how individual components relate to the larger mechanical system.

02 / Documentation

CAD is only part of the engineering communication problem.

After modeling, I created detail drawings for components and subassemblies and compiled them into a design report. This was where I learned how to properly define features, apply GD&T, and create a Bill of Materials.
