01 / Project LogAug 2026 — Present

Pi Car Pro
Custom Gripper

An ongoing personal engineering project to redesign the manipulator on a Pi Car Pro mobile robotics platform. The goal is to move beyond the stock arm and develop a more capable, sturdy, and manufacturable gripper.

STATUS
IN PROGRESS
FOCUS
Mechanical Design / Robotics
01 / Starting Point

Understand the stock platform before redesigning it.

I started by assembling the platform and arm without powering the servos. Before creating custom geometry, I wanted to understand the mounting interfaces, available space, and existing range of motion.

02 / First Observations

The stock mechanism already has useful capability—but also useful problems.

During assembly I identified a shoulder mounting issue where a screw head interferes with the servo and forces the aluminum bracket to bend. I also recorded the stock motion as a baseline before changing the design.

03 / Requirements

Measure first. Set concrete goals second.

The next design phase is based on measurements of the stock arm and chassis rather than guessing at dimensions. The current goals are to improve the gripper, add two additional fingers, and make the mechanism sturdy while remaining practical to manufacture and assemble.

NEXT DESIGN TASKS
  • Measure stock arm reach, motion, and mounting interfaces.
  • Establish target grip geometry and range of motion.
  • Calculate required joint torque.
  • Design a custom mounting interface to the Pi Car.
  • Develop CAD iterations before committing to fabrication.
04 / Project Log

Documenting the project as it happens.

The log is intentionally closer to an engineering notebook than a polished retrospective. Failed ideas, awkward assembly problems, and small observations are useful data for the next revision.

08.22.26

Started with the stock arm as a reference and began researching ways to improve the gripper, potentially adding two more fingers. Created a priority list covering CAD, torque calculations, mounting, electrical architecture, controls, and documentation.

Platform assembly
